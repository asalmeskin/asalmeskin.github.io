About

Curious across modalities.
Rigorous about evidence.

I study how intelligent systems communicate what they know, how faithfully their explanations reflect their internal behavior, and how language and vision models can be evaluated beyond surface-level accuracy.

I am particularly interested in building benchmarks, methods, and evaluation frameworks that make AI systems more reliable for people who use them in research, education, and decision-making.
